    Getting started

    1. Sign in at geoguessr.com in this browser.
    2. Start a solo game and open GeoHelpr from the toolbar.
    3. Play on the GeoGuessr tab — new rounds sync from the page instantly.

    What you’ll see

    • Map — OpenStreetMap preview of the round location.
    • Nearest town — largest nearby settlement.
    • Position in country — region + compass area within the country.
    • Elevation — metres above sea level (Open-Meteo).
    • Distance to capital — km to that country’s capital city.
    • Your guess — distance error and score after you guess (when the API provides it).
    • Tips tab — Plonkit clues, refreshed each round.

    Fair play

    GeoHelpr is for educational solo practice. Avoid use in ranked, competitive, or multiplayer modes.
